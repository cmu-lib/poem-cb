### Making POEM

<i>Project on Open and Evolving Metaliteracies</i> (POEM) is an iterative, peer-reviewed, curated, bilingual collection of reusable and remixable open educational resources designed to enhance a range of information literacies in learners of high school age and above.

POEM is build using [CollectionBuilder](https://collectionbuilder.github.io/), an open source framework for metadata-driven static websites.
